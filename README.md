Overview: Design of a microwave RFIC with an integrated VCO, integer-N PLL, and IQ modulator for use in the 2.4 GHz ISM band, targeting applications such as WiFi 4 (802.11n) or Bluetooth.

Area Estimate: 350 um x 300 um (estimate)  
Pin count: Power 4  Ground 4  Digital 4  Analog 4 (estimate)

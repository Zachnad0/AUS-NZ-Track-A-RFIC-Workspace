**Key resources for phase-8 north hauls:**
- risers up the **x181–204 / x288–397 / x406–472** columns (clear of M2+M3);
- horizontal lanes in the **y273–287.5** top band (clear of M2 and M3), or — once the
  core is placed low enough — in the **empty die channel above the core**
  (DIEAREA y > 287.5+dy), which is unoccupied.

  to the x288–397 column before rising).
